Methodology

A scheduled script downloads official public datasets, cleans them, and publishes plain HTML pages. Nothing is edited by hand.

Sources

  • County of San Diego open data portal: data.sandiegocounty.gov
  • City of San Diego open data portal: data.sandiego.gov
  • California State Controller, USAspending.gov, RTFH, HUD

Each page states the dataset name and the date it was pulled. Categories and department names can change between years; where items were combined or renamed, the page says so.

A figure shown next to another figure (for example, a donation beside a contract) does not by itself show wrongdoing.

Independent public-interest project. Not affiliated with any government. Read-only: this site collects no data.
